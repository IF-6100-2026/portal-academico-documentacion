## Brand & Style

The design system projects institutional authority, warmth, and immediate clarity. Built specifically for parents, guardians, and legal tutors, the visual language balances the seriousness of academic governance with the reassurance required by family members tracking student progress, administrative requests, and school communications.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate** with **Humanist Minimalism**:
- **Trust and Rigor:** Deep navy tones and crisp structural grids anchor administrative weight.
- **Clarity over Density:** Generous white space, explicit typography hierarchies, and scannable summaries remove academic jargon and cognitive clutter.
- **Calm Accessibility:** High-contrast text, clear tactile tap targets (minimum 48px), and discernible status indicators ensure seamless usage on mobile devices while commuting or managing household routines.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for high legibility, institutional credibility, and immediate contextual feedback across mobile viewports:

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Slate 900):** Anchors primary navigation, structural headers, high-emphasis icons, and definitive CTA buttons. Conveys stability, authority, and permanence.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A8A` - Blue 900):** Drives interactive links, selected tab states, progress indicators, and active academic filters.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Emerald 600):** Dedicated to positive academic status: passed competencies, confirmed attendance, paid fees, and "Publicado" badges.
- **Warning / Alert Accent (`#D97706` - Amber 600):** Signifies pending reviews, teacher observations, attendance alerts, and "Pendiente" or "Consultado" milestones.
- **Critical Accent (`#DC2626` - Red 600):** Reserved strictly for overdue documents, academic risks, or unexcused absences.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`):** A clean, crisp slate-tinted foundation. Surface containers rely on subtle boundary borders (`#E2E8F0`) and muted text layers (`#64748B`) to keep student evaluations effortlessly readable in sunlight or dim conditions.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is used globally across display, body, and UI labeling. Its wide apertures, geometric balance, and tall x-height guarantee effortless scanning on mobile devices, even when viewing complex numerical grade matrices or long institutional notices.

- **Headlines:** Set in weights 600 and 700 with slight negative tracking to produce clean, confident section markers. Mobile viewports scale large headings down to prevent unintended line breaks on long student names or dual-term academic titles.
- **Body:** Standardized at 14px and 16px with relaxed line heights (`1.42` to `1.5`) for readable notices, teacher comments, and circular announcements.
- **Labels & Badges:** Use weights 600 and 700 with subtle letter-spacing for micro-metadata, date stamps, and status chips, ensuring quick recognition at a glance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is mobile-first, prioritizing thumb-zone interaction and clear vertical rhythm:

- **Mobile (<768px):** A single-column fluid flow with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Crucial operational elements (such as student pick-up authorizations, latest grades, and urgent notices) stack vertically with `space-md` gaps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Allows side-by-side display of the student profile summary alongside the evaluation stream.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1280px`, framed by `3rem` margins and `2rem` gutters. The parent portal expands into a structured dashboard with a persistent left-hand sub-navigation and consolidated multi-student switchers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and faux 3D styling, adopting instead a refined **tonal layering with soft ambient diffusion**:

- **Base Surface (Level 0):** Background screen `#F8FAFC`.
- **Card & Sheet Surface (Level 1):** Solid white `#FFFFFF` paired with a delicate border `1px solid #E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive / Focus Surface (Level 2):** Elevated cards, dropdown menus, and child-switcher popovers use `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with an accent tint on border focus.
- **Floating Modals & Action Sheets (Level 3):** Modal overlays and bottom sheets use a deep ambient shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` coupled with a dimming backdrop overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)`).

## Shapes

A medium rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`) gives the interface a friendly, humane presence without compromising its institutional structure:

- **Cards, Panels & Containers:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` (12px–16px) border-radius, creating soft framing for dense information.
- **Buttons & Input Fields:** `0.5rem` (8px) radius, providing a crisp, recognizable clickable surface.
- **Chips & Status Tags:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`) to visually distinguish dynamic metadata from interactive cards and square actions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, height 48px on mobile for accessibility. Subtle active state `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E3A8A`, border `1px solid #DBEAFE`. Used for secondary workflows like "Descargar Boleta" or "Ver Historial".
- **Outline / Neutral:** Background transparent, text `#334155`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Publicado (Published):** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`. Includes a solid emerald dot icon prefix.
- **Consultado (Viewed/Acknowledged):** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
- **Pendiente (Pending Action):** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Observación / Urgente:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.

### Academic Summary Cards
- White background with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded at `1rem`. Internal header features the subject title in `headline-sm`, teacher name in `body-sm` (`#64748B`), followed by a split section showing the current grade indicator, attendance percentage badge, and submission date.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Minimum 48px height, `0.5rem` corner radius, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus state transitions border to `#1E3A8A` with a 2px offset ring `rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.15)`. Helper text and validation states appear below the field in 12px weight.

### Student Switcher (Selector de Alumno)
- Persistent horizontal selector at the top of the mobile viewport. Includes a circular avatar with initials, student first name, and current grade level (`e.g., 4° Primaria - Grupo B`). Active student tab displays a solid `#0F172A` pill or underline indicator.

### Attendance & Observation List Items
- Clean row layout separated by `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Features a left-aligned colored date badge (Day/Month), a central column with the incident or subject name, and a right-aligned action icon (chevron or detail toggle) with a 44px minimum tap target.
